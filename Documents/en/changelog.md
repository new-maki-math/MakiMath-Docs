# v1.2.0-beta

Kayson 2026-11-01 02:27

- Lecture details now support printing local and online lectures, with a choice of section, chapter or whole lecture, and light or dark theme.
- Print layouts include a cover, chapter pages and section page breaks, with an option to hide automatically added section titles.
- Print covers randomly display mathematical graphics, body pages have a light watermark, and pagination for tables and content boxes has been improved.
- Improved the knowledge point window's expansion animation in the knowledge library.
- Reorganized frontend modules and editing state handling, and consolidated deployment scripts.
