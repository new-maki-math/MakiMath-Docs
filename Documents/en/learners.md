# Why don't I need to sign in?

Because reading and learning should not start with a sign-in barrier.

On MakiMath, you can read public lectures, look up knowledge points, explore prerequisites and mark what you have mastered. If you want to organize your own material, you can create local lectures, reference knowledge points from the library and add your own understanding. None of this requires an account.

Local lectures and mastery records are currently stored in your browser. When you mark a knowledge point as mastered in one lecture, that mark also appears in other lectures referencing the same point, because they refer to the same library entry. Sharing these marks across lectures does not require signing in, but they do not yet sync automatically to other devices.

“Saved locally” means the data stays in the browser you are using. If you switch browsers or devices, or clear the site's data, you may no longer be able to access your original local content. Export important lectures as JSON regularly to keep a copy. Copying a local lecture's URL alone does not transfer the lecture itself.

To save your work on the server, submit a lecture for review or help administer the platform, you need to sign in and have the appropriate permissions. Accounts identify who maintains content, who submits changes and who reviews them. Signing in does not automatically grant these permissions or upload your local data.

You can start using MakiMath first and sign in when you need online authoring and sharing.
