# Course & Lecture Details

The details page is the overview you see when opening a lecture. It shows the lecture's name, summary, chapters and sections so you can understand what it covers and choose a section to read.

When you first encounter a lecture, you can browse its table of contents to see where the author starts and what they emphasize, and decide whether it suits you.

For lectures in the workshop, click “Add to My Bookshelf” to download a local copy to your current browser so you can continue reading or make your own changes. Editing the local copy does not affect the author's online lecture, and later updates from the author will not automatically overwrite your copy.

To read on paper or keep a PDF, use the print feature to select a section, a chapter or the entire lecture, and choose a light or dark appearance. Once preparation is complete, the browser opens its print dialog, where you can choose a printer or “Save as PDF”. The printed content includes only the lecture text and related headings, without site navigation or action buttons.
