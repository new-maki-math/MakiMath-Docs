# Knowledge Library

The knowledge library is the foundation of MakiMath. It holds the definitions, theorems, formulas and other content maintained centrally by the platform. Different lectures can reference the same knowledge point.

## Find knowledge and prerequisites

You can select a knowledge library and browse by category, or search directly for a knowledge point. Open a point to read its content and prerequisites. If the content is difficult to understand, follow its prerequisite relationships back to the foundations.

Categories help you find content in a particular area, while prerequisites describe relationships between knowledge points. Points in the same category do not necessarily depend on one another.

## View your mastery

You can mark each knowledge point as mastered or remove that mark. The library uses colors to show your mastery across its content, helping you see what is familiar and where gaps remain.

Your mark belongs to the knowledge point in the library. When you mark it as mastered in any lecture, other lectures referencing the same point also show it as mastered. You do not need to record it again for every lecture. These records are currently stored in your browser and do not yet sync across devices.

## Use knowledge points in lectures

Knowledge point details provide reference code that you can copy into a lecture's source. You can also use “Add to my lecture” to choose a chapter and section in an existing lecture and insert a reference.

A reference does not create an independent copy of the content. After an administrator updates a public knowledge point, lectures use the updated content the next time they read the reference.
