# My Bookshelf

My Bookshelf brings your lectures together so you can continue reading without finding them again each time.

Local lectures are stored in your browser. After signing in, you can also see versions of your own online lectures. Working drafts, review snapshots and downloaded local copies are kept separately; they are not one automatically synchronized piece of content.

A local lecture's URL only locates data in your current browser. Sending the URL to someone else does not transfer the lecture. To move content to another device, use the [lecture workbench's import and export feature](./workbench.md#local-and-online-authoring) to transfer it as a JSON file.
