# Workshop

The workshop features lectures submitted by **registered creators** and **shared publicly after approval through review**. You can see how different creators organize a course and choose material that suits your background and goals.

There need not be just one way to teach a mathematical subject. Some lectures focus on definitions and proofs, while others emphasize examples and applications. Before choosing, open a lecture's details to read its summary and table of contents, then decide where to start.

To add your own notes or reorganize the material, download a lecture as a local copy. Subsequent changes affect only your copy, leaving the author's online content unchanged.
