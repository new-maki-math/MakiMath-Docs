# Reading Lectures

The reading page displays the full content of a section. Unlike looking up an individual knowledge point in the library, it lets you follow the author's sequence and understand how the knowledge connects step by step.

## Read content and switch sections

Content can include text, formulas, tables, worked examples and teaching boxes for different purposes. Use the table of contents to jump to a section, or continue with the previous and next section controls.

If you are trying to solve a specific problem, you do not have to read the entire lecture from the beginning. Find the relevant section first, then revisit the foundations as needed.

## Keep mastery records across lectures

Public knowledge point references in lectures are connected to the knowledge library. You can mark mastery on a knowledge point box. When you move to another lecture, the same knowledge point retains that status.

Different authors can explain things differently while referencing the same public knowledge. This lets you focus on new explanations and material you have not yet mastered.
